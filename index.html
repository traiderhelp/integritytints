Why Window Film?

The Florida Sun is Relentless.
Your Windows Don't Have to Be.

Heat Rejection

Block up to 85% of solar heat from entering your space. Keep your home or office cool without overloading your AC.

UV Protection

Block up to 99% of harmful UV rays. Protect your family, employees, furniture, and flooring from sun damage and fading.

Energy Savings

Reduce your cooling costs by up to 30%. Window film pays for itself through lower energy bills — especially in Florida's intense heat.

Safety & Security

Security film holds shattered glass in place during storms, break-ins, or accidents. An invisible layer of protection for your property.

Privacy & Glare

Enjoy natural light without the glare on your screens. Add daytime privacy without closing your blinds or curtains.

Decorative Film

Add style and character to any glass surface. Frosted, patterned, and custom decorative films for homes and businesses.

FAQ

Common Questions

Most residential installations are completed in 2–4 hours depending on the number and size of windows. Commercial projects vary based on scope — we'll provide a time estimate during your consultation.

Window film typically takes 15–30 days to fully cure in Florida's climate. During this time, you may notice small water pockets or a slightly hazy appearance — this is completely normal and will clear up as the film dries.

All of our installations come with a lifetime manufacturer warranty. This covers defects in the film such as peeling, bubbling, cracking, and delamination. We stand behind every installation we do.

Not at all! Window film comes in a wide range of shades and tints. We carry everything from nearly invisible films that block heat and UV while maintaining full clarity, to darker options for maximum privacy. During your consultation, we'll show you samples so you can choose the perfect level.

We're fully aware of Florida's sea turtle lighting regulations. For coastal residential properties, we install films that meet the required 35% VLT (Visible Light Transmission) standard to help reduce light pollution that affects nesting sea turtles.
